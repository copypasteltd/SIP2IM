# 2026-11-21 工作日志 / Maintenance log

## 浏览器挂断与音频背压 / Browser hangup and audio backpressure

- 同一管理员会话可以在本地音频断开后读回原通话并明确确认挂断。返回拨号页先重新读取；缺少新鲜、匹配的信令状态不提交。已受理或结果未知的挂断不因切页自动补发；迟到回复不会覆盖本地未知记录。DTMF 仍需本地音频与桥接状态。
- 浏览器先发送媒体鉴权文本，再按 WebSocket 可见的 4 KiB 应用数据上限决定是否提交新音频块。溢出时推进序号并丢弃该块，不在本地保存以便补发。错误或断开只通知一次，不自动重连。
- Hangup can be explicitly confirmed against fresh readback of the original call even without local audio. In-memory accepted or uncertain outcomes survive same-session navigation and block another submission. A late response does not overwrite an abandoned or timed-out outcome. DTMF still requires a bridged call and local audio.
- Media sends authentication first and caps admission of application data visible via WebSocket at 4 KiB. A dropped chunk advances the sequence and is not replayed. Failure notifies the consumer once without automatic reconnection.

- Core 961 项通过、6 项跳过；Web 200 项通过、聚焦回归 39 项通过。两端类型和构建检查、Web lint 与三语键比对通过。最终构建通过 12 项合成演示冒烟测试；保留 MP4 和 GIF 重新完整解码。

- 演示不启用拨号或挂断、不访问麦克风，既有录屏不展示本轮挂断确认。未操作真机、SIM、真实账号、实库或生产服务，不能用测试结果确认设备已经挂断或有双向音频。
- The demo does not enable dialing, hangup, or microphone access; its original recording does not show the new confirmation. Physical-device behavior, end-to-end audio, live accounts, and PostgreSQL acceptance remain separate.
