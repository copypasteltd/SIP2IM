## Android 队头确认超时退避 / Android outbox ACK timeout backoff

- 握手仅重置普通连接退避。同一绑定与原事件的 ACK 超时跨连接累计，按 1、2、4、8、16、30 秒递增；重连使用普通退避与队头退避中的较长值，最多 30 秒。状态只保留于当前服务实例。
- 匹配的正向 ACK 且确认删除恰好一条原记录，或成功读到不同/空队头，才清除记忆退避。负向、错配、旧连接确认及删除不明均不推进队列。定时回调只影响自己的在途尝试；仍须确认持久代次释放才重连。
- A successful hello resets transport backoff, not the service-local streak for the same binding and event. Reconnect takes the longer delay, capped at 30 seconds. A matching positive ACK with a confirmed one-row deletion or a successful changed/empty head read clears that streak.
- Stale timers cannot retire a replacement socket. Only confirmed generation release permits reconnect, and terminal observations are preserved. Retried events keep their ID, payload, and timestamp; this does not skip a head or resend an SMS command.
